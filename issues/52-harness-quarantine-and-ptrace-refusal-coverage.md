**What to build:** rejected agent work can no longer be published or checked out by accident, and the verifier's refusal to run tests on a kernel without `ptrace` restrictions is proven by a test. Resolves TD-059 (MEDIUM, SECURITY, after an approved risk reassessment) and TD-060 (LOW, VERIFICATION) in `docs/technical-debt.md`. This changes the harness, so it is reviewed and merged outside Ralph (status `ready-for-human`): a sandboxed agent must not author its own trust boundary.
